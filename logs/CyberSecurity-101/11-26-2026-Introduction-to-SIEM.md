# Introduction to SIEM - [11-26-2026]

## What This Covers
This lab provides an introduction to SIEM and explains its importance in security operations.

## Key Concepts
* Understanding the different types of logs and the complexity of reviewing them without a SIEM.
* How different log formats are ingested and normalized.
* True positives and false positives.
* Reading a SIEM dashboard and understanding its usefulness.


## What I Learned

I learned how different log types and formats can make collection difficult and that logs are often collected individually. SIEM centralizes logs in one place and sends alerts when certain conditions are met. It also helps analysts determine whether an alert is a true or false positive and decide how to respond.
