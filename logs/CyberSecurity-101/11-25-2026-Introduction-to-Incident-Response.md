# Introduction to Incident Response - [11-25-2026]

## What this covers

This lab covers the concept of incident response and the framework given by NIST and SANS. Basic concept of playbooks and runbooks.

## Key concepts

* Understanding the need for incident response
* Types and examples of cyber incidents, such as malware infection and insider threat
* Incident response frameworks provided by NIST and SANS.
 NIST phases: Preparation, Detection and Analysis, Containment, Eradication and Recovery, and Post-Incident Activity
 SANS phases: Preparation, Identification, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, and Lessons Learned
* Basic concept of playbooks and Runbooks


## What I Learned

I learned about how important incident response is and the importance of the frameworks. I learned how organizations follow incident response frameworks. I learned how playbooks and runbooks guide response activities and help teams perform them consistently
